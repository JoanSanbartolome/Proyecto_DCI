# Design Technologies & Methods

This document details the hardware technologies and PCB design methodologies applied to the custom STM32F746ZG board to ensure electrical performance, signal integrity, and manufacturing compliance.

---

## ⚡ Applied Technologies

### 1. Reduced Media Independent Interface (RMII)
*   **Description:** A synchronous digital interface connecting the STM32F746ZG internal MAC to the external SMSC LAN8742A PHY.
*   **Implementation:** 
    *   Saves GPIO pins by reducing the interface from 17 pins (standard MII) to 9 pins.
    *   Operates with a shared **50 MHz reference clock** (`RMII_REF_CLK`) generated by the PHY and routed to the MCU (Pin PA1).
    *   Signals are routed with a $50\,\Omega$ single-ended characteristic impedance.

### 2. High-Impedance Active Analog Input Conditioning
*   **Description:** Industrial 0-10V analog signals are scaled and buffered for compatibility with the STM32's 12-bit SAR ADC (3.3V max).
*   **Implementation:**
    *   **Voltage Division:** Scaled using a configurable resistor divider ($100\,\text{k}\Omega$ / $220\,\text{k}\Omega$) providing a scaling factor of $0.6875$ (producing 0-3.125V from a 0-10V input, leaving headroom below the 3.3V rail).
    *   **Unity-Gain Buffering:** Two **OPA350EA/250** Rail-to-Rail operational amplifiers prevent the ADC's dynamic sampling switch-cap network from loading the input signal.
    *   **Power Rail Clamping:** The Op-Amp is powered by the 3.3V analog rail (AVDD), which inherently clamps any input overvoltage (e.g., transients above 10V) to prevent MCU damage.

### 3. CAN Split Termination & Slope Control
*   **Description:** Termination network and EMI suppression on the dual CAN Bus.
*   **Implementation:**
    *   **Split Termination:** Uses two series $60\,\Omega$ resistors (totaling $120\,\Omega$) with a $4.7\,\text{pF}$ capacitor connected from the center tap to GND. This forms a low-pass filter for common-mode noise, significantly reducing RF emissions.
    *   **Slope Control:** The SN65HVD230Q's pin 8 (RS) is grounded via a resistor (or directly to GND for high-speed operation) to limit the slew rate of the CAN outputs, further reducing EMI.

### 4. Low-Capacitance Transient Voltage Suppression (TVS)
*   **Description:** ESD protection for high-speed and general-purpose interfaces.
*   **Implementation:**
    *   **USBLC6-4SC6:** 4-channel protection with ultra-low input capacitance ($3.5\,\text{pF}$) protecting the USB and Ethernet MDI lines without distorting high-frequency digital edges.
    *   **PESD2CANFD24V-TR:** Low-capacitance double diode array specifically designed to protect CAN transceiver buses against ESD transients up to 30 kV.

### 5. Low-Dropout (LDO) Linear Regulation
*   **Description:** Step-down regulation for low-noise rails.
*   **Implementation:**
    *   The **LD39050PU33R** regulates 5V USB power to a clean 3.3V system rail with high Ripple Rejection (PSRR) and ultra-low dropout (typ. $80\,\text{mV}$ at 500mA load), preventing switching noise from compromising the ADC and MCU performance.

## 📐 Used Methods

### 1. Complex Hierarchical Design
*   **Method:** Schematic partitioning using Altium Designer's hierarchical blocks.
*   **Implementation:**
    *   The top-level sheet (`STM32_TOP.SchDoc`) acts as a map referencing nested sub-sheets (`MCU.SchDoc`, `Ethernet.SchDoc`, `CAN.SchDoc`, `ADC.SchDoc`, etc.) via `Sheet Symbols` and `Sheet Entries`.
    *   The Analog Input stage is modularized inside a single sheet (`ADC.SchDoc`) and instantiated twice via the **Repeat** directive (`Repeat(CANAL, 1, 2)`), ensuring identical routing, matching component parameters, and easier layout replication.

### 2. Controlled Differential Impedance Routing
*   **Method:** Routing critical communication buses as coupled differential pairs to prevent signal reflection.
*   **Implementation:**
    *   **Ethernet MDI (TX+/TX-, RX+/RX-):** Routed with a **$100\,\Omega$ differential** target impedance.
    *   **USB Data (D+/D-):** Routed with a **$90\,\Omega$ differential** target impedance.
    *   **CAN Bus (CANH/CANL):** Routed with a **$120\,\Omega$ differential** target impedance.
    *   **Verification:** Trace width and gap spacing are calculated using Altium’s Layer Stack Manager according to the 4-layer PCB stack-up.

### 3. Crosstalk & Digital-Analog Separation
*   **Method:** Physical isolation of noise-sensitive analog tracks from fast digital switching lines.
*   **Implementation:**
    *   **3W Rule:** Applied between the fast digital track `RMII_RXD1` (pin PC5) and adjacent analog traces `ADC12_IN8`/`ADC12_IN9` (pins PB0/PB1) to reduce crosstalk due to capacitive coupling.
    *   **Filter Placement:** Passive RC low-pass filters (1k$\Omega$ / 100nF) are placed immediately adjacent to the MCU pins to shunt any high-frequency noise directly to GND before it can reach the ADC.

### 4. Copper Keep-Out under Ethernet Magnetics
*   **Method:** Discontinuity of copper planes beneath inductive parts.
*   **Implementation:**
    *   All copper layers (Signal, Power, and Ground planes) are completely removed underneath the RJ45 magnetic transformer and connector footprint.
    *   **Rationale:** Prevents common-mode noise on the Ethernet cable from coupling capacitively back into the internal GND/PWR planes, preserving electrical isolation.

### 5. Split-Plane Grounding (Digital vs. Analog GND)
*   **Method:** Partitioning the ground return currents into separate domains.
*   **Implementation:**
    *   A dedicated analog ground plane (**AGND**) is routed underneath the ADC and conditioning circuitry, while a digital ground (**GND**) covers the MCU and high-speed peripherals.
    *   The two planes are joined at a single point (star connection/bridge) near the MCU power input, preventing noisy digital return currents from flowing through the sensitive analog return path.
